How to Sync the UCD Pre-Med Calendar to Your Phone

Never miss an event, clinical workshop, or guest lecture. Connect our master Google Calendar feed with your personal device.

Open in Google Calendar
1

Google Calendar App

Click Subscribe on Google Calendar. When prompted, select "Add Calendar" to sync all updates automatically to your Google account.

2

Apple Calendar (iPhone & Mac)

Download the .ics file or tap to subscribe in Apple Calendar to receive push notifications for upcoming pre-med dates.

3

Outlook & Canvas

Use Subscribe from Web in Outlook or overlay alongside your course schedule to keep track of key quarterly dates.
